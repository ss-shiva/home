About Me

I am a protein designer who closes the loop myself. I redesigned insoluble mouse Metrnl by ProteinMPNN inverse folding while preserving the predicted fold and all five native disulfide bonds, ranked the candidates computationally, then cloned, expressed and purified them and confirmed activity in a cell-based assay.

I designed a de novo solubility tag that yields a glycation-resistant insulin proteoform, covered by an issued patent, and published the first structural models of the Meteorin family as first and co-corresponding author. Six peer-reviewed articles so far.

I am based in Baltimore, MD, and looking for a postdoctoral position in protein design, proteomics or structural biology.
