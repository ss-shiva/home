Design, build, measure. One person, the whole loop.

I am a protein designer who closes the loop myself. I redesigned insoluble mouse Metrnl by ProteinMPNN inverse folding while preserving the predicted fold and all five native disulfide bonds, ranked the candidates computationally, then cloned, expressed and purified them and confirmed activity in a cell-based assay. I designed a de novo solubility tag that yields a glycation-resistant insulin proteoform, covered by an issued patent, and published the first structural models of the Meteorin family as first and co-corresponding author.

02

Recent work

Manuscript in preparation · first author

Rescuing an insoluble protein by inverse folding

Redesigned insoluble mouse Metrnl with ProteinMPNN while preserving the predicted fold and all five native disulfide bonds. Ranked the candidates computationally, then cloned, expressed and purified them. The selected variant expresses soluble in E. coli and retains biological activity.

Fold, assembly state and thermal stability were measured on the purified designs by circular dichroism, SEC-MALS, dynamic light scattering and isothermal titration calorimetry, with interfaces mapped by cross-linking mass spectrometry read against the models.

  • ProteinMPNN
  • 5 disulfides preserved
  • Cell-based activity
  • XL-MS
